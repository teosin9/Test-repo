Download

Three separate downloads — take any or all of them.

1 · Caption file (.srt + .vtt)

The separate caption track of your audio/video. Upload it alongside your video on YouTube, Facebook and most players.

2 · Caption-only video (no background)

Only the captions, on a transparent background — a WebM overlay you can drop on top of any video in an editor. It renders in real time, so a 2-minute clip takes about 2 minutes.

Download caption-only video

3 · Video with captions burned in

Your video with the captions permanently drawn on it, ready to post anywhere. Renders in real time while it plays through once.

Download finished video

Rendered videos download as WebM, which plays in Chrome, Android and most editors. If your editor needs MP4, convert the WebM with any free converter. For audio-only files, the “video with captions” export becomes a caption video on a dark background with your audio.
